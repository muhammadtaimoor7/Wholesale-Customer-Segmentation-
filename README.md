This project uses K-Means Clustering to segment wholesale distribution clients based on their annual spending across product categories such as Fresh food, Milk, Grocery, Frozen items, Detergents and Paper, and Delicassen. Without relying on any predefined labels, the goal is to identify natural client groups, enabling the business to design targeted supply and pricing strategies for each segment.

The Elbow Method was used to determine the optimal number of clusters, resulting in five distinct client segments: grocery focused moderate buyers, fresh food specialists, large grocery and milk buyers, small low volume buyers, and extreme bulk buyers. These insights reveal that clients differ not only in purchase volume but also in purchasing focus, allowing the business to design differentiated supply strategies and pricing models for each group.

Tech Stack

Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Seaborn, Matplotlib
